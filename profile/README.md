**Industrial AI Lab** is a non-profit research laboratory based in Belo Horizonte, Brazil. Our mission is to advance the frontiers of artificial intelligence in industrial applications. We study what AI can and cannot do for the people who keep heavy industry safe, compliant and efficient: auditors, quality and compliance teams, supplier managers and the owners of management systems. We publish everything we build and find in the open, so that others can check it, reuse it and improve on it.

[ailabindustrial.org](https://ailabindustrial.org)

## Research areas

We work where three fields meet:

- **Heavy industry:** process plants, mining, manufacturing, and aerospace, aviation and space.
- **Operational excellence:** audits, regulatory compliance, supplier quality, management systems, lean and safety culture.
- **Artificial intelligence:** language models, retrieval and evaluation methods applied to the documents, standards and evidence these sectors depend on.

**Current programme:** AI support for US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) aircraft certification.

## Principles

- **Reproducible.** Every result ships with the data, code and configuration needed to reproduce it.
- **Open licences.** Code under Apache-2.0, data under CC BY 4.0 or CC0, text under CC BY 4.0.
- **Honest reporting.** We publish null and negative results, and we state the limitations of our work.
- **Disclosed AI use.** We state where and how AI systems were used to produce each output.
- **Disclosed verification.** When a result was checked only by AI, with no human expert review, we say so explicitly.

## How releases work

- **One repository per item.** Each dataset, benchmark, tool, paper or report has its own repository in this organisation. No monorepos.
- **Archived with a DOI.** Each release is archived on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org) and receives a persistent DOI.
- **Citable.** Each repository includes a `CITATION.cff` file, so GitHub's "Cite this repository" button gives a ready-made citation.
- **Versioned.** Releases are tagged; the DOI identifies the exact version you used.
